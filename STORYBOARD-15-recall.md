# STORYBOARD — Film 15 `15-recall` (19.0 s, 1080×1920, 60 fps)

> **Built to the Ahmed Academy reel standard, rev. 1.** That is *a* standard in this
> studio's repertoire — not the only one, and not a law of the internet. See
> `README.md` §7 for the rules and for the standards it deliberately leaves alive.

> **Timing note.** The tables below are the **19.0 s** cut as briefed. The shipped
> version is **38.4 s** after four rounds of feedback: the opening-half pacing gradient
> (`SLOW_OPEN 3.60 → SLOW_TAIL 2.30`) and a bed cut from `0.0s` rather than a 19 s
> window. Story, shot order, copy and claims are unchanged; only tempo and duration moved.
> Current measurements are in `GAUNTLET-15-LEDGER.md`.

Cuts land on **measured onsets** of the 19 s bed (140.625 BPM, beat 0.4267 s):
`2.212 · 2.436 · 3.321 · 5.156 · 6.820 · 9.625 · 10.809 · 12.836 · 13.284 · 14.564 · 16.303 · 17.028`

**Persistent actor:** the *sheet* — a paper note. Born as the page being re-read,
it becomes the blank recall card, the chip on the rail, the card that returns on
day 3, the one card left out of 48, and the thing that flies through the paper.
It is handed over at exact pixel coordinates: the last frame of shot *n* equals the
first frame of shot *n+1* for the sheet.

**Persistent rail:** one gold rule, built in S5, straightened into the spaced-
repetition axis in S7.

## Signature transformations (describable without naming an effect)

1. **The page you are reading becomes the blank card you must fill from memory.**
2. **One card becomes four — the same card, returned on days 1, 3, 7 and 14.**
3. **The three proof lines fold into the logo, and the logo becomes the button.**

## Composition-type coverage (the "don't repeat" check)

macro-typographic · overhead plan · foreground fly-through · colour-field type
impact · UI macro · structural rail · station diagram · overhead timeline ·
count/collapse · UI close-up · portrait+list · stack→one · end card.
Thirteen types, no type used twice. None is "heading above three cards", which is
what reels 11–14 repeated four times.

## Reading-target audit

Never more than two live reading targets: the sheet/actor plus one caption. In S4
(prompt + answer) and S9 (question + tick) the second target only appears after the
first has landed, and leaves before the next arrives.
